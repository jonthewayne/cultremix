# CultRemix --- Product Vision, Architecture & Build Plan

## 1. Product Thesis

**Culture itself is a remix.**

Every artist, designer, musician, filmmaker, writer, brand, and movement
builds on what came before it. We borrow ideas, reinterpret aesthetics,
combine influences, change context, and make things our own. We stand on
the shoulders of the people who created before us, and culture moves
forward because each generation remixes what it inherits.

The saying often attributed to Pablo Picasso captures the spirit:
**"Good artists copy, great artists steal."** The point isn't literal
copying. Great creative work absorbs influences so completely that they
become the raw material for something new.

AI changes the scale at which this can happen.

Take a great typography shirt. Historically, a designer creates one
phrase in one visual treatment and sells that finished design.

**But what if that finished design isn't the end of the creative
process? What if it's just the beginning?**

That same composition, typography, texture, illustration, and visual
language could work with thousands of different phrases. One person
might love the design but want completely different words. **Another
might change the phrase and accidentally create the version that goes
viral.**

**One great creative idea can become the starting point for thousands of
others.**

Two technologies have now matured at exactly the right time to make that
possible at scale.

**Generative AI dramatically expands what we can create and how quickly
we can create it. At the same time, modern print-on-demand has become a
high-quality, globally scalable manufacturing and fulfillment layer,
allowing each new creation to become a real physical product without
producing or holding inventory in advance.**

Now, we no longer have to manufacture one static interpretation of an
idea and hope everyone wants the same thing.

**We can embrace infinite remixing.**

A creator can make a great design and define which parts other people
can make their own. **In a typography design, for example, someone could
remix the words and create a new variation.** In other works, the
remixable elements might be images, colors, characters, graphics, or
other parts of the creative system. A sufficiently transformative
interpretation can fork into an entirely new creative root with its own
remixable system.

**Every finished CultRemix, whether an original or a variation,
immediately becomes a real product that can be purchased, shared,
promoted, and sold on CultRemix.com, with automatic syndication to major
commerce channels such as TikTok Shop, Shop, Amazon, Etsy, and other
supported channels.**

And CultRemix remembers where all of it came from.

> **CultRemix is a creative commerce platform where every work remembers
> where it came from and everyone who contributes to its value can
> participate in its economics.**

Creators publish remixable works. Other people create variations or fork
them into new creative roots. Brands can open portions of their identity
to remixing. Affiliates distribute the resulting products.

**CultRemix is built on the Monad blockchain, using permanent onchain
provenance and programmable royalties to preserve creative lineage and
ensure value flows back to the people who helped create it.**

### Core Principle

> **Don't just make something people like. Make something people want to
> make their own.**

A successful CultRemix isn't merely something that gets likes. It
inspires participation.

**"I love that." becomes "I want my version."**

## 2. Product Vocabulary

-   **CultRemix:** a published creative work and the remixable creative
    system around it. Everything published on the platform is a
    CultRemix because, at the highest level, we're all remixing culture.
-   **Variation:** a new expression made within an existing CultRemix's
    permitted remix system.
-   **Fork:** starts with an existing CultRemix but changes the creative
    system sufficiently to establish a new CultRemix family while
    permanently acknowledging its parent.
-   **Creator:** the person or entity responsible for a CultRemix root.
-   **Variation Creator:** the person responsible for a particular
    variation.
-   **Upstream Creator:** the creator of the direct parent CultRemix
    when a new root is forked. The upstream creator can participate
    economically in that fork and its variations.
-   **Brand / Rights Holder:** an optional entity whose authorized brand
    assets or creative world participate in a CultRemix.
-   **Affiliate:** the person attributed with causing a qualifying
    purchase or creator subscription.
-   **Remix:** primarily the action. **↻ REMIX THIS** ordinarily creates
    a variation; a substantial transformation can become a fork.

## 3. CultRemix Identity

Every published work receives a permanent CultRemix number.

**CULTREMIX № 701-001**\
**AKA "Death Before Decaf"**

`701` identifies the creative family. `001` identifies its first
published expression. The **AKA is an optional human-friendly title**
that gives the work a fun creative identity.

A variation might become:

**CULTREMIX № 701-002**\
**AKA "Sleep Is for Quitters"**

The number can travel across CultRemix.com, merchandise, social media,
commerce channels, Drops, provenance records, and future labels or
packaging.

## 4. Creative Graph, Forks & Provenance

If `CR 701-042` is transformed enough to establish a new creative
system, it might become:

**CR 936-001 --- Forked from CR 701-042**

CR 936 can produce `936-002`, `936-003`, etc. Someone can later fork 936
into `CR 1204-001`.

The ancestry remains visible: **1204 → 936 → 701**

> **CultRemix remembers where creativity came from.**

Creative lineage can continue indefinitely. Economic inheritance should
not.

If Sarah creates CR 701 and Jon forks it into CR 936, Sarah becomes the
upstream creator of 936 and participates economically in the 936 family.
If Mike later forks 936 into CR 1204, Jon becomes upstream creator of
1204. Sarah remains visible in the ancestry, but her upstream economics
do not automatically cascade into 1204.

> **Upstream creator economics travel one fork generation. Provenance
> travels forever.**

For V1, original/variation/fork classification can operate largely on
the **honor system**. Automated lineage detection can come later using
multimodal similarity and human review.

Lineage can also be corrected later. Monad preserves both the original
provenance event and later amendments rather than silently rewriting
history.

## 5. Creative Economics

CultRemix uses a configurable **Creator Pool** rather than stacking
unlimited creator royalties.

A transaction can include: - **Brand Cut** --- optional rights/licensing
economics - **Creator Pool** --- shared among applicable creative
contributors - **Affiliate Cut** --- optional distribution economics -
**CultRemix Cut** --- platform economics - **Transaction Costs** ---
POD, shipping, payment processing, channel fees, refunds, etc.

Creator Pool allocation: - **Original:** creator receives 100% of
Creator Pool. - **Variation:** creator + variation creator divide
Creator Pool. - **Fork:** upstream creator + current creator divide
Creator Pool. - **Variation of Fork:** upstream creator + current
creator + variation creator divide Creator Pool.

The split need not be equal. The key constraint is that all creative
shares come from one bounded Creator Pool.

> **CultRemix makes creative attribution programmable.**

## 6. Creator Studio

The Creator Studio is a core CultRemix experience. It should feel
**fast, visual, iterative, playful, and creative**, not like filling out
an ecommerce listing.

Core loop:

**GENERATE → REACT → FAVORITE → BRANCH → ITERATE → NARROW → CHOOSE**

A creator can begin with: - a prompt - an image - one or more
influences - an existing CultRemix - eventually an authorized Brand
World

Underlying model: - Creative Session - Generation - Parent Generation -
Branch - Favorite - Finalist - Selected Final

### V1 Studio UX

Use a highly visual generation stream with branching and favorites
rather than a generic chatbot. Natural-language direction remains
available. Any generation can be favorited, rejected, regenerated,
branched, compared, or revisited. A persistent Favorites tray helps
narrow experiments into finalists and ultimately:

**MAKE THIS MY CULTREMIX**

A spatial/infinite canvas can come later without changing the underlying
session model.

## 7. Remixable Structure

After selecting the final creative, CultRemix helps define what other
people can change.

For typography this might include headline, subtitle, and color. For
other works it might include images, colors, characters, graphics, or
other defined elements.

AI can suggest the structure. The creator confirms or adjusts it
quickly.

## 8. Product Catalog, Preview & Publishing

CultRemix controls a curated catalog of high-quality POD products rather
than exposing thousands of supplier SKUs.

Possible initial products: - premium heavyweight tee - oversized tee -
sweatshirt - hoodie - poster

V1 can start with only one or a few excellent products.

CultRemix automatically displays the creative across eligible products
and colors. AI can recommend strong combinations and uncheck weak ones
by default; the creator can override.

Publishing should be fast:

1.  **Remixability** --- confirm what others can change.
2.  **Products** --- review products/colors.
3.  **Publish** --- review CR number, optional AKA, description, tags,
    lineage, economics, and product preview.

Then CultRemix automatically prepares production artwork, creates
product variants and Shopify data, creates the public CultRemix page,
records attribution/lineage, registers provenance on Monad, makes the
work purchasable, and makes permitted elements remixable.

## 9. Product Studio & Marketing Studio

CultRemix should develop a recognizable editorial photography language
rather than generic POD mockups.

AI can create: - hero editorial imagery - clean product mockups -
garment details - marketplace thumbnails - vertical product imagery

CultRemix can control the default aesthetic centrally at first; creators
can gain more art-direction control later.

### Marketing Studio --- Post-V1

Later, CultRemix can generate TikTok/Reels concepts, vertical videos,
animated reveals, hooks/captions, Stories, Pinterest assets, thumbnails,
affiliate creative, creator-story scripts, descriptions, and SEO
metadata.

## 10. Creator Subscriptions & Credits

Commercial publishing requires a creator subscription. Creators
subscribe to an **AI-powered creative business studio**.

Possible tiers: - Creator - Creator Pro - Studio

Credits primarily pay for AI operations that cost CultRemix money: -
image generation/editing - AI product photography - future video
generation - future audio/music generation - AI marketing assets

Ordinary platform behavior generally should not consume credits.
Publishing, selling, following, and receiving royalties do not burn
credits.

## 11. Social Network

CultRemix is a social creative network, not simply an AI design utility
or ecommerce store.

Profiles can show: - originals - variations - forks - likes - saved
work - backed work - activity - followers/following - creator status

People can be worth following because they make great things or because
they consistently discover great things.

### Feed

The feed can include new originals, variations, forks, likes, Backs, and
activity from followed users. Remixing itself becomes social content.

### Heart + Rating

Heart means **I like this**. After hearting, a subtle optional 1--10
rating bar can appear.

### Bookmark + Rating

Bookmark means **I want to remember this** and can surface the same
rating bar. There is one personal rating per user per CultRemix.

### Back

Back means:

> **I think this deserves to break out.**

Backing is deliberately scarce, perhaps three per week or fewer, cannot
be purchased, and is effectively an **11/10**. Monad can timestamp the
Back so early support is provable.

V1 Backing is a social/cultural signal, **not an investment product**.

## 12. Affiliate & Creator Referral Economics

Affiliates can earn from both commerce and creator acquisition.

### Product Referrals

An affiliate causes a qualifying product sale and receives the
applicable commission.

### Creator Referrals

An affiliate causes someone to become a paying creator and receives a
**recurring share of eligible creator subscription revenue**.

Percentage and duration remain configurable, e.g. 20% for 12 months.
Referral economics are direct, not multilevel.

### Future-Purchase Attribution

Affiliate programs can optionally attribute **future qualifying
purchases from a referred customer** to the original affiliate for a
configurable duration: first purchase only, 30 days, 12 months, or
another admin-defined period.

## 13. Affiliate Links, Codes & Programs

Creators and affiliates receive tracking links and short promo codes.

Example link: `cultremix.com/?ref=theo`\
Example code: **THEO**

Both map to the same affiliate identity. A code can simultaneously
provide a customer discount and establish affiliate attribution.

Creators provide a preferred base name; CultRemix generates and approves
a short available code.

Super Admin controls offers and commissions. Customer discount and
affiliate commission are independent.

Super Admin manages versioned affiliate programs defining customer
benefit, product commission, creator subscription commission, recurring
commission duration, future-purchase attribution duration, attribution
window, promo-code eligibility, auto-apply behavior, eligible
products/categories, eligible subscription plans, and start/end dates.

An explicitly entered affiliate code can override an earlier first-party
tracking-link attribution. External channels can impose their own
attribution rules.

## 14. Unified Earnings Ledger

All earnings use one generalized economic-event ledger.

Potential sources: - Brand royalty - Creator Pool distribution -
affiliate commission - recurring creator-subscription commission -
gifts/tips - future sponsored rewards

Each earning records source, recipient, role, underlying event, gross
amount, costs, distributable amount, currency, attribution source,
applicable configuration version, created date, maturity date, status,
settlement batch, and Monad transaction.

### States

**Pending** --- inside applicable return/refund/chargeback window.\
**Available** --- matured and withdrawable.\
**Paid** --- settled.

Users see a normal balance and can withdraw available earnings.
Automatic payouts can come later. Small earnings can be aggregated into
settlement batches.

## 15. Monad --- Provenance & Settlement Layer

CultRemix is built on the Monad blockchain, but blockchain UX should
remain nearly invisible.

Users sign up normally. CultRemix silently provisions an embedded
wallet.

No MetaMask. No seed phrase. No buying MON. No gas UX.

CultRemix sponsors relevant transaction costs.

Monad anchors: - authorship/provenance - creative lineage - lineage
amendments - relevant economic configuration - scarce Back events -
settlement records

When a work publishes:

**✓ Provenance registered on Monad**

Commerce remains conventional fiat commerce. Matured earnings can settle
in USDC on Monad.

> **Monad is the trust layer underneath CultRemix.**

> **Monad turns the cultural graph into a verifiable economic graph.**

## 16. Brand Worlds & Brand Assets --- Platform Roadmap

Brands can open parts of their identity to remixing.

> **"Here's the part of our identity we're inviting culture to play
> with."**

A Brand World can contain logos, word marks, phrases, characters, visual
systems, likenesses, photography, graphic elements, and other Brand
Assets.

Permissions can be: - Open - Approval Required - Invited Creators -
Closed

Brands can control whether variations/forks are allowed, require
approval, and designate selected community work as **OFFICIAL ✓**.

A brand can occupy both the Brand and Creator economic roles when it
creates the original system itself.

CultRemix can eventually maintain a Brand Asset Registry with
permissions/economics, while clearly distinguishing platform claims from
actual legal trademark ownership.

Future rights systems can add trademark/logo detection, external rights
searches, similarity detection, review workflows, and rights-holder
takedown/reporting processes.

## 17. Drops, Collections, Challenges & Sponsorship --- Platform Roadmap

At scale, abundance requires curation.

-   **Feed:** continuous social creativity.
-   **Collections:** persistent themes such as Typography, Couples,
    Faith, Holidays, Music, Fandom, etc.
-   **Drops:** highly curated editorial selections representing what
    CultRemix believes is exceptional right now.

Drops can become digital lookbooks and commerce collections distributed
across supported channels. Selection gives creators status,
distribution, and commerce exposure while preserving provenance.

Brands can sponsor Drops or creator prize pools **without needing to be
the brand being remixed**. Sponsors can appear tastefully in challenge
pages, share creative, social campaigns, and winner announcements.

CultRemix can eventually run monthly or themed creator pools funded by
CultRemix, sponsors, or community patrons. Avoid paid entry fees
initially.

## 18. Gifts & Creator Support --- Platform Roadmap

Inspired by YouTube/Twitch creator support, CultRemix can support: - Tip
Creator - Gift Studio Credits - Gift Creator Pro - Fund the Next
CultRemix - Sponsor a Drop - Gift a Remix

Support actions can become social activity and flow through the unified
earnings ledger.

## 19. Digital Products & Identity Remixing --- Platform Roadmap

CultRemix is not limited to physical apparel.

### Digital Products

Examples: - low-cost image packs - expanded style packs - digital
portraits - downloadable creative sets

A digital purchase can bring the customer into CultRemix for advanced
generation/customization and can optionally credit some or all of the
digital purchase toward physical merchandise.

### Identity Remixing

Future creative primitives include: - couples - friends - families -
pets - holiday portraits - fantasy-romance covers - K-pop-style groups -

Typography and graphic apparel are the first primitive, not the limit of

## 20. Worlds, Video, Music & Authorized Experiences --- Long-Term Expansion

A CultRemix can eventually become more than an image.

A K-pop-style world, for example, could produce: - poster - digital
image set - custom song - animated/video experience - physical
merchandise

Official Worlds can be authorized by musicians, athletes, celebrities,
creators, brands, and entertainment properties.

Fans could enter an artist's visual world while the rights holder
controls approved source material, permissions, economics, and official
status.

This is a long-term expansion, not V1 scope.

## 21. Community Backing & Token Ideas --- Deferred Exploration

CultRemix has explored the idea of letting the community fund emerging
culture **and get rewarded for identifying it early**.

The social Back mechanic is intentionally included first as scarce
cultural conviction.

Any future mechanism involving financial returns, revenue participation,
a CultRemix token, or investment-like backing requires substantially
more product, economic, and regulatory thought.

Do not make these dependencies of V1.

## 22. Commerce & Distribution Architecture

CultRemix is a **headless Shopify commerce experience**.

CultRemix.com owns: - discovery - social experience - Creator Studio -
remixing - publishing - product presentation - profiles - lineage -
earnings experience

Shopify provides: - product/variant commerce infrastructure - cart -
checkout - Shop Pay - orders - taxes - ecosystem/channel integrations

POD handles manufacturing and fulfillment.

Finished products can eventually syndicate automatically to supported
commerce channels such as: - TikTok Shop - Shop - Amazon - Etsy - other
supported channels

A finished variation can therefore be a normal SKU on an external
channel even though the remixing experience itself lives on
CultRemix.com.

**Creativity becomes inventory. Inventory leads people back to
creativity.**

## 23. Technical Architecture

### Frontend / Web App

**TanStack Start**, deployed on **Cloudflare**.

The experience should feel fluid and app-like while preserving real
URLs, deep links, SEO, sharing, and direct navigation.

### Backend

**Convex** is the primary hosted application backend, not merely a
database.

Use Convex for: - database - queries - mutations - transactional
application logic - actions - scheduled jobs - realtime subscriptions -
social/feed state - creator sessions - economics/ledger state -
integration orchestration where appropriate

The existing Better Auth + Convex starter can provide authentication.

Because Convex is a hosted backend service, **Cloudflare Hyperdrive is
not required** for the Convex connection. Hyperdrive would matter if
Workers were directly connecting to Postgres/MySQL.

### Media Storage

**Cloudflare R2** for generated images, production artwork, product
assets, and future video/audio.

### AI

**Vercel AI Gateway** from the Cloudflare-hosted application/backend.
The app does not need to run on Vercel or Next.js.

Use a model-independent architecture so image/video/audio providers can
evolve.

### Commerce

**Shopify Headless**.

### POD

One high-quality POD provider for V1. Do not build a multi-provider
abstraction before the first real fulfillment path works.

### Blockchain

**Monad** for provenance, lineage, selected social proofs, and
settlement.

### Settlement Currency

**USDC on Monad**.

### Wallet UX

Embedded wallets with sponsored gas so users do not need MON or crypto
knowledge.

## 25. V1 --- Hackathon / Working Product

V1 is being built first as a **Monad Social, Attention & Culture
hackathon project** and should be ruthlessly limited to proving the core
loop.

### First Creative Primitive

**Typography + graphic apparel**

### V1 Must Prove

A real user can:

1.  Sign up with normal authentication.
2.  Receive an invisible embedded wallet.
3.  Create a profile.
4.  Follow other users.
5.  Browse a simple social/discovery feed.
6.  Heart, bookmark, and optionally rate CultRemixes.
7.  Use a scarce Back.
8.  Enter the Creator Studio.
9.  Generate typography/graphic-apparel concepts.
10. Iterate, branch, favorite, and select a final.
11. Define simple remixable fields.
12. Publish an original CultRemix.
13. Receive a CR number and optional AKA.
14. Register provenance on Monad.
15. View the CultRemix on a real product.
16. Remix an existing CultRemix into a variation.
17. Preserve creator/variation attribution.
18. Declare a fork when appropriate.
19. See basic lineage.
20. Add a real product to cart.
21. Ideally complete a real Shopify/Shop Pay purchase.
22. Ideally send the order through actual POD fulfillment.
23. Attribute the economic event correctly.
24. Show creator/affiliate earnings as Pending/Available/Paid.
25. Demonstrate USDC settlement on Monad.

### V1 Affiliate Scope

At minimum: - tracking link attribution - short affiliate code -
configurable discount - product commission - recurring
creator-subscription referral model represented in the architecture

If time allows, make recurring subscription attribution fully live.

### V1 Social Scope

At minimum: - profiles - follow - feed - heart - bookmark - optional
rating - scarce Back - original/variation/fork activity

### V1 Creative Scope

Do **not** build couples, K-pop, video, music, Brand Worlds, or complex
identity replacement before the typography/graphic-apparel loop is
excellent.

### V1 Principle

**The grand vision defines the architecture, not the V1 scope.**

The V1 loop needs to be real, beautiful, and understandable end-to-end.

## 28. Implementation Principles

1.  **Plan the architecture before coding.**
2.  **Build vertical slices, not disconnected backend layers.**
3.  Every stage should end in something runnable and testable.
4.  Keep the blockchain invisible unless its trust properties matter.
5.  Do not build future features merely because the schema anticipates
    them.
6.  Prefer configurable/versioned economics over hard-coded percentages.
7.  Preserve provenance rather than rewriting history.
8.  Keep publishing fast.
9.  Make the Creator Studio feel creative, not administrative.
10. Use AI credits primarily where CultRemix incurs AI cost.
11. Keep commerce conventional and familiar to consumers.
12. Make social participation native to creation, discovery, and
    distribution.
13. Optimize V1 for a stranger being able to understand the product
    without explanation.

## 30. The Product in One Loop

**DISCOVER → LOVE → REMIX → CREATE → PUBLISH → SELL → ATTRIBUTE → EARN →
DISCOVER AGAIN**

A person sees something they love.

They make it theirs.

Their version becomes a real product.

Someone else discovers that version.

Maybe they buy it.

Maybe they remix it.

Maybe they fork it into something new.

CultRemix remembers everyone who contributed along the way and gives the
people who create, evolve, and distribute culture a way to participate
in the value they help create.

**Culture itself is a remix. CultRemix makes that remixable,
attributable, social, and economic.**
