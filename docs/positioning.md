# CultRemix positioning

The official description of CultRemix (Jon, October 29, 2026). Use this wording for the site, pitch decks, applications, social bios, and press. Cut shorter versions from it rather than rewriting it.

## The idea

Culture itself is a remix. Every artist, brand, and movement builds on what came before. But commerce still treats a finished design as the end of the creative process. What if it was just the beginning? One great idea could spark thousands of versions, and the one that goes viral might be someone else's remix.

## What CultRemix is

CultRemix is a social creative commerce platform powered by AI, the Monad blockchain, print-on-demand fulfillment, and community. Creators choose what others can change; every work and remix becomes a real product with no inventory, and every sale pays back through the work's lineage, recorded on Monad.

## Tagline

CultRemix — remix culture, get paid.
